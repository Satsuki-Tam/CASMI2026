# 041 pre-registration: is the popularity prior too strong for hidden class 2? (written 14:12 JST, before computing)

Evidence so far (computed before this note): (1) when a COCONUT-truth C2 molecule is still missed by the v7a combination (dev2 full_eval, 52 of 119), the rank-1 decoy is usually structurally unrelated (median Morgan Tanimoto to the truth 0.19, 79% same formula) and beats the truth mainly through the prior (median pop difference +4.0; train decoys +5.6, PubChem decoys win on GL instead); (2) development truths are unusually well known: median popularity percentile 0.79 within their C2 shortlist, 44% in the top 10% (panel truths are the most popular candidate in every case), except D (single-library NP) and G (synthetic) whose truths are unpopular. Hidden class-2 truths have no public spectra by definition, which plausibly correlates with lower popularity, so the development sets may overstate the prior's value (as the forum reported for curated-DB membership flags).
