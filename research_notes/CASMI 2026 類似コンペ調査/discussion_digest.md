### 1.2 Test set, metric, scorer, data updates

- **Test-set facts (data page):** ~1,500 spectra of ~400 molecules, 1–16 spectra/molecule (median 3), 100% Bruker timsTOF, monoisotopic mass 157–1,159 Da (median 348), 10 adducts, minimal cleaning (precursor-inconsistent spectra dropped, base peak < 1,000 raw counts dropped, peaks > precursor + 2 Da removed), class distribution hidden. Visible `test.parquet` is a dummy made of training spectra.
- **No ion mobility:** "Its deliberately excluded; the test set has no CCS values." (Healey 09-27, 743368); "the test set does not include 1/K0 or CCS. It has the same columns as provided in test.parquet." (Killian 09-28, 743569).
- **Class definitions:** "Class 3 is absent from both PubChem and COCONUT." (Healey 09-23, 742274); "By definition, class 3 structures are not in PubChem at all and must be predicted de novo." (Killian 10-13, 748552). Breakdown refused: "We will not provide the breakdown of the test set, but our hope is that there are enough novel structures to make it worth working on them." (Killian 10-05, 745758). Public/private split: "We are not revealing details ... participants should expect members of all three classes in both sets." (Killian 10-13, 748882). The LB page shows a 31%/69% public/private split (quoted by WEI ZIHE12 748882 and yves mouaha 750572) → ~125 public molecules if split by molecule.
- **09-15 Healey (741404):** public LB "is calculated not on the contents of the test.parquet downloadable dummy file (which ... just contains a sample of Enveda-180 spectra ...) but rather on the public split of the Hidden test set provided to your submission notebook."
- **09-15 inversion (741471):** "a small update to the training data, adding water loss adducts to some of the samples. It is recommended that you re-download the file."
- **09-19 inversion (741851):** scoring notebook linked from the Evaluation page (`metric/casmi-mean-reciprocal-rank`).
- **09-23 Healey (742274) scorer edge cases:** "Empty entries are discarded before ranks are assigned. A;;B is read as two guesses, so B is at rank 2, not rank 3. A trailing ; is not a guess and does not affect your score".
- **09-28 Killian (742265):** "The live scorer is identical to the published casmi-mean-reciprocal-rank v13. It has not changed. We are investigating the concerns raised in the thread you referenced [742088: filler SMILES → 0.000]." P100 GPUs sunsetted (T4/other only). **10-13 Killian (748882):** "Adding valid, distinct guesses after an unchanged rank-1 guess will not lower that row's MRR score, provided the submission passes validation and is scored successfully."
- **09-30 Healey (744556):** "Yes, solutions are stereo-stripped before canonicalization." ## 3. Validation insights

### 3.1 Why naive CV fails
- **enveda-np-examples is not a holdout.** Its 250 structures carry 55,599 spectra in 8 other libraries (riken, gnps, mona, massbank, pluskal_ms2, msdial, drug_plus, masaryk); holding out by `ingest_lib` or a random 2% structure split leaves them in the library → CV 0.33–0.37 vs LB 0.15–0.16. Test: run library search on the holdout and require zero exact InChIKey14 self-matches (best cosine 0.000). — haideptry (741597, 09-16); DancingLumberjack & alex chilton (742055, 09-19) [FACT]
- **Fingerprint models that look better offline do nothing on LB:** two FPNets with hard-negative top-1 0.694/0.640 on a random 2% structure split scored 0.330 vs 0.335 (worse) — near-identical molecules leak across random splits. — starkhushi (742055) [FACT]
- **Library molecules are too "popular".** PubChem popularity puts the answer #1 for 67% of np-examples molecules but ~0% of enveda-180 molecules without public spectra (top-25: 99% vs 0.7%); a popularity prior gave +0.50 MRR locally but +0.075 on LB (0.146 → 0.221). — Aryan (748068, 748524) [FACT]. Same trap from the fusion side: locking the v4 engine's confident picks lifted np-examples validation 0.63 → 0.89 but LB 0.413 → 0.409 — "well-documented training structures ... do not behave like the test's class 2". — YahiaZakaria20 (745952) [FACT]
- **Curated-DB membership flags** look great on public-library holdouts and hurt on LB. — Udam (743254) [FACT]
- **MassSpecGym memorisation:** ICEBERG rerank +0.02 on class 2 overall, ≈0 on non-MSG structures. — Bull (748524) [FACT]
- **Spectra-per-molecule mismatch:** public test copy averages 3.03 spectra/molecule, typical holdouts 4.3–5.0; one aggregation feature was worth +0.019 at own counts but +0.0065 ± 0.0033 count-matched (5 draws); mixing the two scales under-predicted a submission 4×; a single resampled draw is worth ±0.011 → average ≥ 5 draws; capping k at available spectra drags the realised mean (2.5 vs 3.03). — DancingLumberjack (742055) [FACT]
- **Novelty by deletion overstates reach ~3×** vs truly absent molecules. — Udam (743254); echoed by Mansur, Bull (751300) [FACT]
- **Tuning on the evaluation panel inflated an effect 2.8×** (+0.065 → +0.023 ± 0.007 on four held-out panels, t 3.45, n 1000; LB 0.341 → 0.336). — NeckBeard (743254) [FACT]
- **"AUC on a selected cohort is not MRR":** a feature with 80.3% truth-above-isomer separation moved 4 molecules of 249; decoy controls must differ only in the tested property. — alex chilton (742055) [FACT]
- **Public-notebook lineage never CV-validated** (0.373 → 0.436: two-engine fusion, popularity prior, `rel > 250` PubChem promotion justified by `# Skomuro LB 0.403 Rule`, dropping ICEBERG judged "+0.013" and "−0.015" in different versions, tail backfilling); fpnet_full1 trained on all of train so the pipeline can't be validated on held-out train. — Bull (748068, 10-10) [FACT]; "Most of our 'small improvements' on top of strong pipelines ended up slightly worse than the untouched version." — Aryan (749908)

### 3.4 Where the MRR goes (failure taxonomies, class-2 condition)
- **DancingLumberjack (742055):** of 250 held-out NPs not ranked first: truth absent from candidates 4 (1.6%, +0.016 headroom; 2 formula mismatches, 1 outside ±10 ppm, 1 not in COCONUT); truth at ranks 2–25: 100 (40%, +0.274); truth > 25: 6 (2.4%, +0.024) → **ranking beats recall 17:1**. The truth's analog score is already ~90% of the best candidate's (median), only 2% of failures are analog-poor; the winner is usually NOT a close analogue (median Tanimoto 0.48, 20% > 0.7); 36 truths at rank 2, 23 at rank 3. Decomposition: rank-1 0.580, rank-2-only 0.070, ranks 3–25-only 0.051; truth in top 25 for 96%; 73% of ceiling extracted (742088).
- **alex chilton (742055):** 249 NPs: shipped MRR 0.7315 → same-formula blockers removed 0.9869 → oracle 1.000 ⇒ **perfect same-formula separation = 95.1% of the gap; everything else incl. recall = 0.013**. In rank-2 cases 40 of 42 blockers have the exact formula and 69% a different Murcko scaffold.
- **Victor alexandre (742088), LB probes on a 0.324 analog-propagation/HistGBR fork:** library twin only → 0.275; ranker's #2 only → 0.055; ranks 3–25 promoted → 0.059; so the top-25 holds the answer for ≈40% of public molecules and the pipeline extracts 81% of that ceiling (leaders at 0.36x ≈ 90% or a bigger pool); reordering ranks 2–25 by Tanimoto-to-twin tied at 0.324 while tail sets differed in ~90% of rows → blend signals.
- **Antriksh (749412), masked probes on E006 (0.440):** engine-2 top-1 only where it disagrees with the final #1 → 0.023 (~3 public molecules), E006's #1 on those rows → 0.102 (~13); engine 1 and the PubChem channel hold ~1 buried answer each.

### 3.5 LB noise, split, shrinkage
- Public ≈ 31% of ~400 molecules (~125–132; Bull, Antriksh, WEI ZIHE12, yves mouaha). One molecule miss → rank 1 ≈ +0.008 public (≈ 0.0025 full). SE of a difference between two submissions ≈ 0.016 (Udam) ⇒ < ~0.03 end-to-end is invisible; others: < 0.006 unmeasurable from one submission (DancingLumberjack), ±0.006 ranker seed (prvsiyan, Victor), ±0.003 GPU nondeterminism (Aryan), band 0.318–0.327 across several near-identical submissions (DancingLumberjack).
- Offline → LB shrinkage: direction transfers, size does not (Aryan: +0.14 CV ⇒ +0.03–0.04 LB; S2 0.502 → 0.627 ⇒ +0.03). Panel gains shrink by dilution (~1/3 of molecules isomer-limited) and overlap (Udam). Full-count CV +0.006 ⇒ LB +0.008 when scales are matched (DancingLumberjack).
- PubChem-only molecules in the test appear to be few: fixed-rank PubChem insertion +0.01–0.02 locally, LB 0.295 → 0.288 (Bull); PubChem as full source +0.002, tail-only +0.001 (DancingLumberjack) [FACT drops, SPEC inference].

## 6. Isomer discrimination

- **The problem is same-formula ranking, not formula or coverage:** 98% of DB-route misses share the formula (Udam); 95.1% of the class-2 gap is same-formula separation (alex chilton); >95% correct formula (Adarsh); ~66 of ~83 window candidates share the truth's formula in train+COCONUT, ~10,000 candidates in PubChem windows (alex chilton, Truthseeking); 40 of 42 rank-2 blockers have the exact formula and 69% a different Murcko scaffold; median Tanimoto(truth, winner) 0.48–0.49 (DancingLumberjack, alex chilton).
- **What has measured positive effect:** in-silico fragmentation with explained intensity as a within-molecule relative feature in a learned ranker (Aryan S2 +0.125, LB +0.03; Udam; Truthseeking 16.7% vs 8.9% control); parsimony-weighted MetFrag-lite (fewest cleavages, 1-bond ≫ 2-bond, linear intensity, 0.005 Da, H −2..+3: NeckBeard +0.023 held-out); analog/Tanimoto propagation (haideptry; Aryan +0.033 S2); FPNet logit scoring (haideptry); GBDT/LambdaRank over ~30 features; diversity from an independent engine (Aryan +0.001–0.002; Antriksh +0.002).
- **What has measured null/negative effect:** the same MetFrag-lite channel when measured marginally against an already strong ranker (+0.0025, alex chilton; forced-choice at chance, starkhushi) — i.e. fragmentation's value depends on how much the ranker already captures; connectivity scoring, formula/sub-formula gates, collision-energy depth, base-neutral-loss features, ring-cut infeasibility, ICEBERG feature, DreaMS similarity on blocked pairs (alex chilton); hand-written cleavage chemistry, 3-bond cuts (NeckBeard); popularity prior on non-library molecules (Aryan); LLM reasoning (Adarsh); CCS is unavailable (host).
- **Dev metric:** same-formula isomer panel with paired t-tests across several libraries, never tuned on the evaluation panel; report marginal gain against the full ranker with an OOF control that reads +0.0000 (Udam, NeckBeard, alex chilton). "A real separation on a selected cohort, then nothing once it competes with the ranker's own probability."
- **Unvalidated ideas:** fragment-formula annotation and plausibility given parent formula / CE / fragment population priors (Adarsh, hengck23, symmys); forward models (CFM-ID 4, ICEBERG-MSG, ICEBERG atlas) for candidate-spectrum comparison — allowed, value unproven on LB; contrastive spectrum–fingerprint learning (haideptry).

## 8. Ten most actionable, non-obvious insights for a gold-zone (~0.48 public) score

1. **Match the scorer exactly and apply the electron fix to train.** Metric key = stereo-stripped, tautomer-canonical InChIKey14 (RDKit 2026.03.3); the shipped `inchikey14` differs for 4.4% of NP structures and ~8% of truths have tautomer twins. The hidden test's positive-mode m/z were corrected by one electron mass; train enveda-180 and the Zenodo release were not — subtract 0.000548 Da there before any sub-0.01 Da matching (742042, 743254, 744556, 748882).
2. **Treat the public LB as a ~125-molecule, ±0.016-SE instrument and never submit anything unparseable.** One molecule ≈ 0.008; GPU/seed noise ±0.006; filler SMILES zeroed whole submissions twice; never hard-code 400 molecules (a failing assert silently rescores your previous file); pin the Docker image (unpinned copy lost 0.014) (748524, 743254, 742088, 749412, 749908).
3. **Use masked LB probes to measure channels instead of guessing.** One real guess per molecule + a never-matching placeholder (`CCO`) elsewhere tells you how many public molecules each engine/channel gets right (library twin 27.5%, ranker #2 5.5%, ranks 3–25 5.9%, engine-2 disagreements 3 vs 13); this is how the +0.002 "engine-2 top-1 at rank 2" fix was found and how the 0.40 reachability ceiling was measured (742088, 749412).
4. **Validate on structures held out InChIKey14-wide from every library, resampled to 3 spectra/molecule, with a no-public-spectra split.** Require zero library self-matches; library-based holdouts overrate popularity priors 7× and confident-pick locking (+0.26 local, −0.004 LB); count-mismatched CV mis-sizes effects 3–4×; average ≥ 5 draws and several seeds; accept a change only if the class-2-like (enveda-180-without-spectra) split improves (742055, 748524, 748068, 745952).
5. **Class-2 points are same-formula ranking, so build structure-aware evidence as relative features inside a learned ranker** — explained intensity of 1–2-bond cuts expressed as the gap to the best candidate of the same molecule (S2 0.502 → 0.627, LB +0.03), parsimony-weighted MetFrag-lite (1-bond ≫ 2-bond, no 3-bond, linear intensity, 0.005 Da, H −2..+3), analog propagation `Σ Sim^4 · Tanimoto` — and measure each marginally against the full ranker with an OOF control; standalone AUCs on selected cohorts have repeatedly translated to nothing (748524, 743254, 742055, 741745).
6. **Diversity of candidates beats more of the same model.** Extra FPNet seeds/banks add ≤ 0.002; fine-tuning on timsTOF (= enveda-180 chemistry) hurt; the only things that moved the 0.437–0.443 lineage were new candidates from an independent engine fused by RRF with the source top-1 protected, and inserting a second engine's top-1 at rank 2 (+0.002 on two bases). Never let a weaker engine take rank 1 (−0.037) (749251, 749412, 745952, 742055).
7. **Candidate pool: COCONUT + training structures first; PubChem (or PubChemLite/ICEBERG atlas) only behind an isomer-capable ranker and gated promotion.** Every public NP collection is 95–100% inside PubChem and adds ~0 truths beyond COCONUT; blind PubChem expansion cost 0.335 → 0.205 and prvsiyan's class-2 0.52 → 0.35; ±5–8.5 ppm suffices on timsTOF; Enveda's adduct labels are clean so skip adduct re-hypothesis on the test, but down-weight the ~12k formate mislabels when training (748552, 742055, 741745, 743254, 742087).
8. **Do not inherit the public lineage blindly, and make every weight you keep reproducible.** Its 0.373 → 0.455 gains were LB-picked without CV (two-engine fusion, popularity prior, `rel > 250/150` PubChem promotion, ICEBERG on/off, tail backfill) and fpnet_full1 is trained on all of train; hosts now require the training code actually used for every weight, including competitor-shared ones and fine-tunes, plus the code that generates intermediates — otherwise retrain with your own published code (748068, 750612, 749217, 751373).
9. **Exploit the allowed toolbox the forum has barely used:** ICEBERG PubChem atlas (91M predicted spectra, usable as an in-silico library with generation code), CFM-ID 4/ISDB, MSG-trained ICEBERG/GLACIER/MIST, DreaMS SSL backbone (as a retrieval probe — though it is below chance on same-formula blocked pairs), MS2DeepScore 2.0, FIORA-OS, TabPFN, PubChem CID-PMID/Patent/SID counts, newer GNPS/MassBank/MoNA dumps and GNPS-NIST14-MATCHES, LOTUS/NPAtlas/HMDB; SIRIUS/CSI:FingerID and anything NIST-trained are out (745134, 743774, 751373, 748882, 745832).
10. **Class 3 is 30–50% of the test and the only headroom, but protect retrieval while attacking it.** Nothing has moved it on the LB beyond +0.004; deletion simulations overstate reach 3×; analog-edit generators can produce the truth for ~100/130 absent molecules locally yet rank it first 7 times — so the work is parent selection, formula-exact generation (FRIGID/DiffMS/DualLGD allowed), and gated insertion below confident DB hits, validated on molecules truly absent from all databases and on an isomer-aware ranker that can score generated decoys (750241, 741659, 743254, 751300, 748029).
