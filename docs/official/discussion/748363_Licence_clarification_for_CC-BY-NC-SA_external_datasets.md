# Licence clarification for CC-BY-NC-SA external datasets
topic 748363 | by **Zaid Wali** (https://www.kaggle.com/zaid216) | posted 2026-10-11 | votes 1 | comments 1 | https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra/discussion/748363

Several widely used public datasets for this competition are licensed CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 (non-commercial, share-alike), and others list "other" or no licence. Rule 6a requires external data to be "publicly available and equally accessible to use by all Participants of the Competition for purposes of the competition at no cost," and rule 5a.3 exempts incompatible-licence data from the winner's open-source grant. Could the host confirm whether CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 datasets, and datasets with "other" or unspecified licences that are freely downloadable on Kaggle, count as compliant external data for prize-eligible submissions?
